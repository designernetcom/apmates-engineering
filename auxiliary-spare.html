PU Hose

Dust filter cartridges are filtration components used in compatible dust collection systems to capture airborne particulate matter from industrial processes. They help support cleaner working environments and effective dust management.

Hopper Magnet

Hopper magnets are designed for installation in compatible material feeding systems to help capture ferrous metal contaminants from flowing raw materials. They can be used in suitable plastic, powder, granule, and other material processing applications.

The appropriate design depends on material flow, hopper dimensions, magnetic separation requirements, and cleaning access.

Injection Moulding Heaters

Injection moulding heaters provide heat to compatible machine components involved in plastic processing. They help maintain the required processing temperature when correctly matched with the machine design and temperature control system.

Hot Air Dryer Heaters

Hot air dryer heaters are used in compatible drying systems to heat the air circulated through a drying chamber or material handling unit. They support drying processes where controlled air temperature is important for material preparation.
