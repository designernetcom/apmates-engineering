Our Industrial Mixers & Blenders

Industrial mixing and blending equipment designed for efficient material mixing, uniform blending, and reliable processing across diverse manufacturing and process industries.

01
INDUSTRIAL MIXER
01 / INDUSTRIAL MIXING SOLUTIONS

Industrial Mixer

Industrial Mixers are used to combine and process different materials according to the requirements of manufacturing and production operations. They can support mixing applications involving powders, granules, liquids, and other compatible materials.

Mixer configuration, capacity, mixing mechanism, and construction materials can be selected according to the product characteristics and process requirements.

Versatile mixing solutions for industrial processes
02
RIBBON BLENDER
02 / POWDER BLENDING

Ribbon Blender

Ribbon Blenders are designed for mixing dry powders and granules using a rotating ribbon agitator. The movement of materials within the mixing chamber helps achieve consistent blending for suitable industrial formulations.

They can be used in food processing, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, and other industries where uniform dry material blending is required.

Consistent blending of powders and granular materials
03
DOUBLE CONE BLENDER
03 / GENTLE POWDER MIXING

Double Cone Blender

Double Cone Blenders use a rotating double-cone vessel to mix dry powders and granules through a tumbling action. This design is suitable for applications requiring gentle blending of compatible materials.

Equipment selection can be based on batch size, material flow characteristics, cleaning requirements, and the desired blending performance.

Gentle tumbling action for dry material blending
04
OCTAGONAL BLENDER
04 / GRANULE BLENDING

Octagonal Blender

Octagonal Blenders are designed for blending powders and granules through the rotation of an octagonal-shaped container. The tumbling movement helps distribute materials throughout the batch.

They are suitable for selected pharmaceutical, chemical, food, and other industrial applications where batch blending is required.

Rotational blending for powders and granules
05
PADDLE MIXER
05 / MECHANICAL MIXING

Paddle Mixer

Paddle Mixers use rotating paddles to move and combine materials within a mixing chamber. Depending on the equipment configuration, they can handle a range of compatible powders, granules, and other process materials.

Paddle arrangement, drive system, vessel dimensions, and construction materials can be chosen to suit the required application and mixing conditions.

Mechanical mixing for diverse industrial materials
06
TUMBLER MIXER
06 / BATCH MIXING SOLUTIONS

Tumbler Mixer

Tumbler Mixers blend compatible powders and granules by rotating a mixing vessel to create continuous material movement. This approach is suitable for applications requiring controlled, low-impact batch mixing.

Vessel design, operating speed, batch capacity, and material compatibility are important considerations when selecting a tumbler mixing system.

Rotational mixing for compatible dry materials
07
SIGMA MIXER
07 / HIGH-VISCOSITY MIXING

Sigma Mixer

Sigma Mixers are designed for intensive mixing and kneading of viscous materials, pastes, and dough-like formulations. Their mixing blades generate mechanical action suited to demanding material processing applications.

Mixer capacity, blade configuration, drive power, and vessel construction can be selected according to the material properties and production requirements.

Kneading and mixing for viscous industrial materials
08
CONTRA MIXER
08 / INTENSIVE MIXING

Contra Mixer

Contra Mixers are designed for mixing applications that require coordinated material movement and effective distribution within the mixing vessel. The configuration can be selected to suit specific industrial processing requirements.

The mixing mechanism, vessel capacity, drive arrangement, and construction material depend on the product being processed and the intended operating conditions.

Mixing equipment for application-specific processing needs
09
PLOUGH MIXER
09 / BULK POWDER PROCESSING

Plough Mixer

Plough Mixers use plough-shaped mixing tools to create intensive movement of powders and granules within a horizontal mixing vessel. This action helps distribute materials and supports a range of dry mixing and processing applications.

Equipment configuration and optional features can be determined according to the material characteristics, required batch size, and process objectives.

Intensive mixing for bulk powders and granular materials
10
BLENDING TANK
10 / LIQUID BLENDING SOLUTIONS

Blending Tank

Blending Tanks are used to combine compatible liquids and other process ingredients within a controlled vessel. They can be incorporated into production systems where consistent mixing and preparation of process batches are required.

Tank capacity, agitator configuration, material of construction, and inlet and outlet connections can be selected according to the product properties and process requirements.

Process tank solutions for liquid mixing and blending
