Our Storage Tanks & Silos

Industrial storage solutions designed for material storage, process handling, and storage requirements across diverse industrial applications.

STORAGE SILOS
01 / BULK MATERIAL STORAGE

Storage Silos

Apmates Engineering Storage Silos provide dedicated storage solutions for bulk materials used in industrial processing and manufacturing facilities. Their design can be selected according to the material being stored, required capacity, site layout, and material handling arrangements.

Suitable silo configurations help organize bulk storage and support material supply to downstream processing equipment.

Storage solutions for industrial bulk material handling

HORIZONTAL TANKS
02 / HORIZONTAL STORAGE SOLUTIONS

Horizontal Storage Tanks

Horizontal Storage Tanks are designed for storing compatible liquids and process materials in industrial environments. Their horizontal configuration can be suitable for installations where tank height, access, and available floor space influence equipment selection.

Tank capacity, construction material, supports, and connections can be determined according to the stored medium and installation requirements.

Horizontal storage configuration for industrial facilities
03
VERTICAL TANKS
03 / VERTICAL STORAGE SOLUTIONS

Vertical Storage Tanks

Vertical Storage Tanks offer a space-conscious solution for storing compatible liquids and process materials. Their upright configuration can help make use of available vertical space in manufacturing plants, processing units, and industrial storage areas.

The tank design and construction can be selected according to storage capacity, material compatibility, operating conditions, and site requirements.

Vertical tank design for efficient use of installation space
04
BALANCE TANKS
04 / PROCESS FLOW MANAGEMENT

Balance Tanks

Balance Tanks are used in process systems to provide temporary holding capacity and help manage variations in material flow between connected equipment. They can support more consistent transfer and coordination between upstream and downstream processing stages.

Capacity, inlet and outlet arrangements, and construction materials should be selected according to the process medium and system requirements.

Supports process flow management between equipment stages
05
BUFFER TANKS
05 / PROCESS BUFFER STORAGE

Buffer Tanks

Buffer Tanks provide intermediate storage within industrial process systems, helping accommodate short-term differences between material supply and demand. They can be incorporated into suitable production lines to support process continuity and coordinated equipment operation.

Tank capacity, material selection, and connection arrangements depend on the application and the characteristics of the stored medium.

Intermediate storage to support process continuity
06
HOLDING TANKS
06 / PROCESS HOLDING SOLUTIONS

Holding Tanks

Holding Tanks are designed to retain compatible liquids or process materials before further processing, transfer, or packaging. They can be integrated into manufacturing and processing facilities according to the required storage period and production workflow.

Construction material, tank capacity, access points, and connections should be matched to the stored product and operational requirements.

Reliable holding capacity for industrial process systems
07
SQUARE TANKS
07 / SPACE-EFFICIENT STORAGE

Square Tanks

Square Tanks provide a practical storage configuration for suitable industrial applications where layout and available installation space are important considerations. Their straight-sided design can make positioning and arranging multiple units convenient in compatible facilities.

Tank dimensions, construction material, and fittings can be selected according to the intended use and storage requirements.

Practical tank configuration for space-conscious installations
08
CHEMICAL STORAGE
08 / CHEMICAL STORAGE SOLUTIONS

Chemical Storage Tanks

Chemical Storage Tanks are designed for storing compatible chemicals and process liquids in industrial facilities. Selection requires careful consideration of the chemical's properties, concentration, operating temperature, and compatibility with the tank construction material.

Appropriate tank design, fittings, and installation provisions help support safe storage and reliable integration into chemical processing systems.

Storage solutions selected for chemical compatibility
